Privacy Policy – Turbo Race: Desert Rally

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Turbo Race: Desert Rally ("the Game") is published by awfpath ("we"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, location or any account information. Game progress (coins, unlocked cars, settings) is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service operated by Unity Technologies (ironSource). LevelPlay runs an auction across several advertising networks for each ad, so the network that ultimately serves an ad may vary.

To show and measure ads, Unity and the participating ad networks may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about how you interact with ads and the Game,
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

Unity's privacy policy is at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy and the LevelPlay (ironSource) policy is at https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/air/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/.

Ad personalisation. By default the Game requests only contextual (non-personalised) ads, which are chosen without building an advertising profile of you. You can opt in to personalised ads at any time from Settings → Personalized Ads in the Game, and switch them back off the same way.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads in your device settings (on Fire tablets: Settings > Privacy > Advertising ID).

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on our servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes all locally saved progress. For data processed by Unity and the ad networks in its mediation, see the policies linked above.

Changes

We may update this policy; the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
