Privacy Policy – Turbo Race: Desert Rally

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Turbo Race: Desert Rally ("the Game") is published by awfpath ("we"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, location or any account information. Game progress (coins, unlocked cars, settings) is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about how you interact with ads and the Game,
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the Game asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the Game. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads in your device settings (on Fire tablets: Settings > Privacy > Advertising ID).

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on our servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes all locally saved progress. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy above.

Changes

We may update this policy; the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
